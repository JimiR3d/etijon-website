OUR STORY

About Us

The Etijon Foundation Inc. is a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit organization dedicated to empowering children, youth, and underserved communities through education, health awareness, and essential support services.

MISSION STATEMENT

To empower underserved children and communities by providing essential educational resources, promoting health and wellness awareness, and fostering sustainable community development.

VISION STATEMENT

A world where every child has access to quality education, proper health resources, and the opportunity to build a dignified and prosperous future.

The legacy of Late Chief/Very Rev. Etim John Ndekhedehe, JP, Ikpoto Oro
WHAT GUIDES US

Our Core Values

INTEGRITY

We operate with complete transparency, honesty, and accountability in all our programs and financial stewardship.

COMPASSION

Driven by genuine care for human dignity, we put community needs at the heart of every initiative we undertake.

EQUITY

We are dedicated to narrowing gaps by ensuring fair access to opportunities, resources, and services for underserved communities.
